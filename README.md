PROJECT OVERVIEW

Delicious Delicacies is a bakery based in Belhar, Cape Town,
established in 2021. This website project serves as the foundation for
establishing a digital presence for the bakery, allowing customers to learn
about the business, browse products, and make enquiries.

The website consists of five pages:

1. Home - Introduction to the bakery, featured products, and links.

2. About Us - The bakery's history, mission, vision, and values.

3. Our Products - A catalogue of products

4. Enquiry - Custom cake request details and general enquiry information

5. Contact - Two locations Belhar and Kuils River, contact details, and
   opening hours.

WEBSITE GOALS AND OBJECTIVES

- Establish Online Presence: Make the bakery discoverable to customers
  searching in Belhar and surrounding areas.

- Showcase Products: Display high-quality images and descriptions of baked
  goods.

- Generate Enquiries: Provide a clear pathway for customers to request
  custom cakes or catering services.

- Build Brand Awareness: Communicate the bakery's story, mission, and
  commitment to quality.

- Provide Essential Information: Share location, contact details, and
  opening hours clearly.

KEY FEATURES AND FUNCTIONALITY

- Responsive Navigation: Consistent menu across all pages linking to each
  section.

- Product Catalogue: Categorised display of cakes, donuts, cupcakes, and treats

- Custom Cake Enquiry: List of required fields for customers to
  prepare their enquiries.

- Two Physical Locations: Main bakery at 17 Keisergracht Street, Belhar,
  and a second location at OK Grocer Highbury Park, Kuils River.

- Google Maps Links: links to view both locations on Google
  Maps.

- Social Media Links: links to Instagram and Facebook.

- HTML: Use of basic header, nav, main, section, and
  footer elements.
