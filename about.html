About Delicious Delicacies

Our Story

Delicious Delicacies was founded in 2021 by baker, Shuaib Salwary. What started as a small home kitchen experiment soon became a beloved local bakery, known for using only the finest ingredients and time‑tested recipes.

Today, we are proud to serve the Belhar community and beyond with freshly baked treats, pastries, and our custom cakes. All our products are made from scratch, with a passion that you can taste in every bite.

Our Mission & Vision

Mission

To delight the Belhar community with freshly baked, high‑quality goods made from the finest ingredients, served with warmth and customer care.

Vision

To be the bakery of choice in Belhar, known for quality, creativity, and community spirit.

What We Promise

  • Quality: We never compromise on ingredients or techniques.
  • Community: We support local suppliers and give back to our neighbourhood.
  • Creativity: Every custom cake is a unique work of art.
